Booking Deletion

Permanent deletion is exceptional and irreversible.

Grant it only to a trusted Admin or Owner who needs to remove an eligible duplicate or test booking. Paid, invoiced, receipted and otherwise retained bookings remain blocked and should be Archive/Void instead.

Every deletion still requires the current login password, exact booking reference, a reason and final confirmation. Granting this permission does not delete a booking.
