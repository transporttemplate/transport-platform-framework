Automation Builder

Each rule needs an enabled template and an explicitly selected SMS channel. Pickup and driver reminders are 12 hours before travel; office reminders are 24 hours. Missed historical windows are skipped. Delays run after the event; quiet hours apply to all sends.

Company SMS templates

Available variables: company_name, booking_reference, customer_name, pickup_date, pickup_time, pickup_address, destination_address, driver_name, driver_phone, google_review_link, document_label, expiry_date. Wrap variables in {{double braces}}. Private notes and fares are never interpolated.

Company SMS preferences

Shared email / SMS notification channels

These are the shared notification channels. Email only, SMS only, Both and Neither are explicit choices; existing values stay unchanged until you save. Email still respects its master switch; unsupported email triggers offer SMS or Neither. Equal quiet-hour times disable the quiet period. Live sending is separately locked by the release gate.

Pairing PIN

One active handset per company. Pairing a replacement revokes the old handset. Use a dedicated business SIM: SMS received on the selected SIM is uploaded to this company.

Consent and opt-outs

Unchecked records an opt-out and cancels queued messages. Inbound STOP also opts out. Review requests always require explicit marketing consent.

Daily counts use Europe/London. Uncertain messages are never retried automatically; check the handset before taking any further action.
