LIVE PAYMENT CONFIGURATION

Changes to Stripe settings may immediately affect customers’ ability to pay online. Verify the company, Stripe account and Test/Live mode before saving.

STRIPE API KEY WARNING

The publishable key must belong to the correct company and match the server-side Stripe account and mode. Never enter a secret key into a browser-visible setting.

ENABLE STRIPE WARNING

Do not enable online payments until the correct company-specific Stripe secret, webhook signing secret and payment configuration have been verified.

DISABLE STRIPE WARNING

Disabling Stripe stops new online payments but does not automatically cancel or reconcile existing payments.

CARD SURCHARGE WARNING

Card surcharges may be prohibited for consumer payments under UK law. Unsupported consumer card surcharges cannot be enabled here.
